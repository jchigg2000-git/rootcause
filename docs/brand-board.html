3. The mark

A segmented hexagon — socket, bolt head — with a diagnostic pulse crossing it. Inline SVG (app/components/logo.tsx), never a raster asset: it inherits colour, scales, and costs no request. The ring is currentColor so it works on the dark masthead and on light surfaces; the pulse is always amber, because it is the one element that must not recede.

4. Colour rules

Amber is a signal, not a surface #FFC107 carries black, not white. Use it for rules, marks, active states, small type on dark, and the hazard stripe. Never as a background behind body copy.
Never recolour an evidence semantic Danger / warn / ok / info are fixed. They are the only colours in the product that carry meaning a reader acts on.
Chrome may vary; content may not Manufacturer livery moves the masthead, stripe, rails and chips. Body surface, type and semantics stay put, so two reports are comparable at a glance.
Contrast floor is 4.5:1 --muted:#5c636e measures 4.83:1 on paper. Do not lighten it. The two values that failed this before — helper text at 3.79:1 and placeholder at 3.14:1 — are fixed.

5. Manufacturer livery

Reports are coloured by the equipment manufacturer. The theme is resolved server-side from the intake Make field and is never model-supplied — it is document chrome, like section numbering. An unrecognised make falls back to house livery.
